HikeSafe 🌲🚑

On-Device, Offline-First AI First-Aid Triage & Outdoor Survival Assistant

📌 Context & Motivation

Social media trends inspire thousands of people to head into the outdoors every day. While exploring nature is incredible, many adventurers hit remote trails unaware of the risks—often without proper preparation or cell coverage.

After sustaining an injury during an orienteering race and experiencing trail helplessness firsthand, I realized a critical safety gap in outdoor technology: most emergency apps fail when you lose signal.

HikeSafe was built to bridge the gap between outdoor adventure and survival safety. It puts a calm, reliable, offline-first medical triage assistant directly into the hands of hikers when connectivity fails and help is far away.

🔑 Key Features

100% Offline-First Architecture: Zero dependence on cell service, Wi-Fi, or cloud infrastructure. All data and logic stay local.

On-Device Quantized AI Model: Fine-tuned lightweight CoreML quantized to run locally on standard smartphones without overheating or draining the battery.

High-Stress UI/UX: High-contrast, minimal-click user interface designed for panic situations, injured fingers, or harsh outdoor lighting.

Instant Triage Guidance: Step-by-step emergency first-aid protocols, trauma management advice, and outdoor survival procedures.

Resource Efficiency: Engineered with a minimal footprint to preserve device power in multi-day off-grid emergencies.

🚀 Getting Started

Installation & Setup

This repository contains the packaged Xcode build ZIP for HikeSafe.

Download: Download the HikeSafe.zip file directly from this repository or the Releases page.

Extract: Unzip the downloaded archive on your Mac.

Run:

Open the unzipped project folder in Xcode.

Select your target iOS simulator or connected device.

Press Cmd + R to build and run the application.

Note: An active internet connection is only needed for the initial Xcode deployment. Once loaded onto the device, HikeSafe operates 100% offline.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

HikeSafe is an emergency assistance tool, not a replacement for professional medical care.

This software is designed to provide actionable guidance when standard emergency services (like 911/112) are out of reach. Users should always notify emergency contacts of their itinerary before entering dead zones and seek professional medical attention as soon as connectivity is restored.
